## Attribution

Attribution means naming the source and linking to it:

    Fastvideo GPU JPEG benchmark, <article URL>, run of <date>.

**Keep the measurement conditions next to the numbers** - the card, the driver,
the library versions, the quality, the subsampling and the measurement mode. A
figure without them is not reproducible.
